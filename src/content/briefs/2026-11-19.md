- [Six Boys and Six Girls From the Rolex Scholastic Junior All-America Team Earn Spots in the Tournament of Champions at TPC San Antonio](https://www.ajga.org/news/2026-rolex-scholastic-junior-all-american-honorees-announced) — AJGA
  The AJGA invited the top six boys and top six girls from its 2026 Rolex Scholastic Junior All-America team, based on the Rolex AJGA Rankings. The boys group includes Andrew Lieu of Dallas, Alexander Zhang of Plano, Texas, and Brian Yu of Palo Alto, Calif., and the girls group includes Jenna Kim of Raleigh, N.C., and Angela Liang of Bridgewater, N.J. Competition on the Canyons Course starts Sunday after Saturday's practice round and wraps Wednesday.

- [Red River Cup Singles Start at 9:30 a.m. at Twin Hills as Texas Chases Its First Cup](https://golfoklahoma.org/oklahoma-looks-to-defend-title-as-rid-river-cup-matches-move-to-twin-hills-cc/) — Golf Oklahoma
  Eighteen singles matches close out the third Red River Cup Matches in Oklahoma City, each worth three points under Nassau scoring for 54 points in all. Oklahoma, the two-time defending champion, keeps the cup on a tie, so Texas needs 41 of the 81 total points to win it outright. Last year Texas led 14.5-12.5 after the four-ball matches, but Oklahoma won 36 of the 54 singles points to retain the cup.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Dixie Senior Amateur Late Entry Runs Through Friday Ahead of the Dec. 9-11 Championship at Eagle Trace](https://www.dixieamateur.com/pages/tournament) — Dixie Amateur
  The 54-hole stroke-play event at Eagle Trace Golf Club in Coral Springs, Fla., has separate Mid-Master (ages 40-54), Senior (55-64) and Super Senior (65 and over) divisions, with a 9 a.m. shotgun start for the final round Dec. 11. The late entry fee of $435 applies through Nov. 20, and tee times and pairings are posted about seven days before the first round. Caddies are not allowed, and players record scores in the Golf Genius app.
  Tags: senior
