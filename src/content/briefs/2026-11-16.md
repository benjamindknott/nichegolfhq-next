- [Rolex Tournament of Champions Is Next for the AJGA at TPC San Antonio, Where Ringkamp and Oh Won Last Year](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/juniorgolfhq/rolex-tournament-of-champions) — nichegolfHQ
  The AJGA's season-ending event plays 72 holes of stroke play Nov. 21-25 on the Canyons Course at TPC San Antonio, set at 7,106 yards for the boys. Luke Ringkamp won the boys title last year at 15-under 273, and Nikki Oh took the girls title at 8-under 280. The event dates to 1978 and has been held at TPC San Antonio since 2022.
  Tags: juniors

- [Keyes and Sass Win the Ewing Cup Four-Ball Wire-to-Wire by Seven Shots at Dallas Athletic Club](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/midamgolfhq/ewing-cup-four-ball/2026-ewing-cup-four-ball-final-recap) — nichegolfHQ
  Robbie Keyes and Michael Sass closed with a 6-under 66 on the Blue Course on Sunday to finish at 18-under 198, seven clear of Michael Randle and Zac Verses. Kolton Crawford and Sean Heidrick tied for third with Ryan Zamorano and Todd McCarroll at 10-under 206, and Steve Paterson and Austin Chung posted the low round of the day, a 7-under 65. Keyes and Sass also qualified for next year's U.S. Amateur Four-Ball.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Stephenson and Sandlin Win the Ewing Cup Senior Division by One With a Closing 68](https://www.golfgenius.com/pages/12440584375098158373) — Golf Genius
  Lewis Stephenson and Lee Sandlin shot a 4-under 68 on the Blue Course at Dallas Athletic Club on Sunday to win the senior division at 11-under 205. John Stollenwerck and Neal Barfield finished one back at 10-under 206, and 36-hole co-leaders Mike Lohner and Todd Vancil took third at 8-under 208 after a 71. Kirk Johnson and Tray Tyner tied for fourth with 2021 champions John Brellenthin and Tim Peterson at 6-under 210.
