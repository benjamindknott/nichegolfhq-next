- [2023 Champion Aphrodite Deng Is Back in the Rolex Tournament of Champions Field as Sunday's Start Nears at TPC San Antonio](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026147/field) — AJGA
  Deng, a 2028 grad from Millburn, N.J., won the girls title at TPC San Antonio in 2023 at 8-under 280 after carrying a five-shot lead into the final round. Grant DeLorenzo of Houston, the 2025 Elite Invitational boys champion, is on the boys list. Competition starts Sunday on the Canyons Course after Saturday's practice round and wraps Wednesday.

- [John Hunter Represents the TGA as Texas and Oklahoma Open the Red River Cup Matches at Twin Hills](https://txga.org/3rd-red-river-cup-set-for-november-18-19/) — Texas Golf Association
  The third Red River Cup Matches begin today at Twin Hills Golf & Country Club in Oklahoma City, with nine four-ball matches Wednesday and 18 singles matches Thursday. Hunter of The Woodlands is one of eight mid-amateurs on the 18-player Texas Golf Association team, and one of five returning from last year's side. Oklahoma has won the first two playings, including 48.5-32.5 in 2025, and retains the cup if the matches finish tied; Texas needs 41 of 81 points to win it for the first time.
  Tags: mid-am

  The 54-hole stroke-play event returns to PGA National Resort in Palm Beach Gardens, Fla. McCoy shot 73-68-73 to win last year's edition at 214, the same total as runner-up Jerry Gunthorpe. Doug Clapp, Gene Elliott and Michael Kelley tied for fourth at 216.
